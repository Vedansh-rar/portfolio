freelance brief

BillCut

An Instagram grid redesign for a debt-management fintech. Topics like EMIs, credit-card dues and interest feel stressful, so I wanted posts people would actually stop and read instead of scrolling past another finance ad.

brief
Instagram 3×3 grid
type
Freelance assignment
made
9 posts, moodboard
tool
Figma
  1. Bold headline first. People scroll fast, so the oversized words (FREE, DEBT, SAVINGS) carry the message before anyone reads the small print.
  2. Warm yellow, strong black, blue to highlight. Approachable instead of scary; red only when a word needs urgency.
  3. A visual hook per post. Broken handcuffs for the EMI cycle, a couple juggling three EMIs, a salary notification. Things people recognise.
  4. Designed as one 3×3 grid. Type-led, people-led and object-led posts alternate so the profile feels varied but clearly one brand.
